WHO WE ARE

A streaming home for curious listeners.

WaveCraft makes it easier to discover, listen to, organize, and enjoy music in one personal place. Browse genres and moods, follow charts, explore artists and albums, build playlists, save Liked Music, and keep your library in order.

Whether you are finding your next favorite song or replaying an old obsession, WaveCraft keeps listening smooth and personal — with a Premium upgrade when you want offline listening and higher-quality audio.
